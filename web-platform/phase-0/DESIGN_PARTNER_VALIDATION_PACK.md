# Cartogen AI Workspace — Design Partner Validation Pack

**Phase:** 0 — discovery and validation
**Status:** Ready for structured interviews
**Primary beachhead:** humanitarian operational mapping

## Research objective

Validate whether a browser-native Cartogen workspace solves a recurring operational problem better than the current desktop-only workflow, and identify the smallest product that design partners would pilot or fund.

## Interview segments

Recruit at least five organizations across:

1. humanitarian GIS/IM teams;
2. NGO implementing partners;
3. emergency-response consultants;
4. public-sector or international-organization GIS teams;
5. specialist GIS teams with shared governance needs.

Interview a mix of:

- GIS analysts;
- GIS managers;
- programme/operations managers;
- ICT/security reviewers;
- procurement or budget owners.

## Interview script

### Context

- What mapping decisions do you make every week?
- Which workflows are recurring rather than one-off?
- Which data sources and formats are involved?
- Who needs to review or consume the output?
- What happens when the main GIS analyst is unavailable?

### Current workflow

- Walk through the last situation map or coverage-gap analysis.
- Which steps happen in QGIS, spreadsheets, scripts, or portals?
- Where do people copy data manually?
- Where do errors or delays occur?
- How long does the workflow take from source data to decision-ready output?

### Collaboration

- Who needs to edit, review, approve, or only view?
- How are versions and comments handled today?
- Do teams work across offices, field locations, or unstable connectivity?
- What should never be visible to another team or external stakeholder?

### Web-workspace concept

Show the prototype and ask:

- What would you expect to do first on this screen?
- Which panel is useful and which is distracting?
- What is missing for your current workflow?
- What would make you trust the AI result?
- When should the system require approval?
- Which export or report would be most valuable?

### Commercial validation

- Would you pilot this with approved non-sensitive or synthetic data?
- Who would approve a pilot?
- What would a successful pilot prove?
- Would you pay for training, implementation, support, managed AI, or the workspace?
- What procurement/security requirements would block adoption?
- What annual budget range is realistic for this problem?

Do not ask leading questions such as “Would you buy this?” without first establishing the current cost and pain.

## Prototype tasks

Ask each participant to attempt:

1. Open a humanitarian coverage-gap project.
2. Find the districts with the largest service gaps.
3. Inspect a facility and its partner-presence context.
4. Ask Cartogen AI for a plan.
5. Review the plan before execution.
6. Produce a draft situation map/report.
7. Share it with a reviewer.
8. Identify what data, privacy, or approval warning they expect.

## Measurement sheet

Record:

- task completion: success / partial / failure;
- time to first meaningful action;
- time to first useful output;
- points of confusion;
- terminology issues;
- trust concerns;
- missing data/connectors;
- collaboration requirements;
- required approvals;
- willingness to pilot;
- willingness to fund;
- follow-up owner and date.

## Validation thresholds

Proceed to Phase 1 when:

- at least five qualified organizations have been interviewed;
- at least three identify the same recurring workflow;
- at least three can provide approved synthetic/non-sensitive pilot data;
- at least two agree to a pilot or design-partner activity;
- one buyer or budget owner confirms a credible funding path;
- no critical security or licensing objection remains unresolved;
- the humanitarian MVP scope is stable enough for engineering estimation.

If these thresholds are not met, revise the product scope before building.
