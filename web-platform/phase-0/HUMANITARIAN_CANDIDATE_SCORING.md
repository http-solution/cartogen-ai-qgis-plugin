# Humanitarian Candidate Scoring Rubric

Score each candidate from 0–2 per category.

## Qualification

- 0: no recurring mapping workflow identified;
- 1: occasional mapping need;
- 2: recurring weekly/operational workflow.

## Workflow fit

- 0: outside initial humanitarian scope;
- 1: adjacent but requires major new capability;
- 2: coverage gap, 3W/4W, access, needs, or situation-map workflow.

## User urgency

- 0: exploratory only;
- 1: interested but no current project;
- 2: active operational pain with a deadline.

## Data readiness

- 0: no approved data path;
- 1: data decision pending;
- 2: synthetic/public/approved non-sensitive data available.

## Pilot authority

- 0: cannot authorize participation;
- 1: can introduce the owner;
- 2: can sponsor or authorize a pilot.

## Funding signal

- 0: no known budget or grant route;
- 1: possible budget/grant/sponsor path;
- 2: budget owner or funded programme identified.

## Strategic value

- 0: isolated use with no reference value;
- 1: useful learning opportunity;
- 2: strong reference, network, or sector learning value.

## Interpretation

- 11–14: priority design partner;
- 8–10: qualify and interview;
- 5–7: nurture or refer to training/content;
- 0–4: do not prioritize for the first pilot wave.

Never let strategic prestige override data safety, workflow fit, or a credible pilot owner.
