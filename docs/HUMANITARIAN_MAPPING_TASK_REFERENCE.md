# Humanitarian Mapping Task Reference

This reference taxonomy defines the humanitarian mapping work Cartogen AI should support. It is used to guide tool coverage, prompt examples, workflow design, acceptance testing, and future QGIS processing features.

## 1. Base mapping and reference data

Base maps; roads, tracks, bridges, paths, waterways; buildings and footprints; settlements, villages, towns, neighbourhoods, facilities, landmarks, land use, land cover, drainage, elevation, slopes, terrain, place names, administrative boundaries, common operational datasets, geographic databases, map indexes, map sheets, map books, atlases, paper-map digitisation, historical-map georeferencing, format conversion, geographic-name validation, data standardisation, and unmapped-area identification.

## 2. Rapid and emergency mapping

Affected areas; damaged and destroyed buildings; blocked roads; floods; landslides; debris; wildfire; earthquake; cyclone and storm impacts; conflict locations; explosions; displacement; evacuation and emergency-access routes; safe and unsafe areas; helicopter landing zones; urgent-assessment areas; situation maps; emergency coordination maps; search-and-rescue maps; pre/post-event comparisons; information-gap maps; rapid damage assessments; daily or weekly emergency updates.

## 3. Population and displacement

Population distribution, density, estimates and projections; affected populations; IDP and refugee settlements; reception centres; spontaneous settlements; host and returnee communities; asylum seekers; migration and displacement routes; origins and destinations; settlement-size change; demographic characteristics; age, sex, disability, older people, children, pregnant women, minority and marginalised groups; pressure on services; underserved populations; displacement dashboards and infographics.

## 4. Camp and settlement mapping

Camp boundaries, blocks, zones, shelters, household units, communal facilities, registration and reception areas, distribution points, water and sanitation, health posts, schools, markets, roads, paths, drainage, waste points, lighting, security infrastructure, firebreaks, emergency access, overcrowding, shelter density, available space, expansion areas, high-risk zones, accessibility barriers, growth, relocation, closure, service distance, master plans, camp profiles, facility inventories.

## 5. Damage and needs assessment

Damage severity; destroyed and partially damaged structures; affected households; damaged schools, hospitals, clinics, water, sanitation, power, telecommunications, warehouses, markets, bridges and roads; inaccessible communities; debris and hazards; district comparisons; affected-population estimates; field-assessment prioritisation; survey and imagery integration; field validation; damage reports; recovery and reconstruction priorities.

## 6. Infrastructure and service mapping

Health, education, WASH, food, shelter, protection, legal assistance, community, logistics, transport, energy, telecommunications, government, partner, cash-assistance, registration and referral facilities; capacity; operating status; opening hours; availability; service areas; coverage gaps; travel times; population served; service-directory and referral outputs.

## 7. Health and public health

Disease cases, suspected cases, outbreaks, clusters, incidence, prevalence, mortality, case fatality, vaccination, immunisation, catchment areas, maternal and child health, nutrition, malnutrition, cholera and waterborne risk, vectors, mosquito breeding, environmental-health risks, sanitation and water access, referrals, ambulance coverage, health workers, medical supply needs, geographic barriers, underserved populations, epidemiological maps, health-risk maps, dashboards, vaccination planning.

## 8. Water, sanitation and hygiene

Water sources, points, boreholes, wells, quality, contamination, trucking, storage tanks, pipelines, treatment, latrines, toilets, bathing, handwashing, waste, drainage, open defecation, sanitation gaps, water-per-person, latrine-to-person ratios, water distance, nonfunctional facilities, repairs, maintenance, hygiene promotion, WASH coverage, inventories, assessments, reports.

## 9. Food security and nutrition

Food insecurity, consumption, distributions, markets, prices, agriculture, crops, livestock, pasture, fishing, drought-affected farmland, supply routes, market access, malnutrition, nutrition treatment, assistance coverage, cash and voucher programmes, access barriers, income and expenditure, food-price trends, affected populations, priority assistance areas, severity maps, nutrition reports, agricultural and market monitoring.

## 10. Logistics and supply chains

Warehouses, distribution centres, ports, airports, border crossings, roads, corridors, bridges, restrictions, fuel stations, vehicle routes, delivery locations, capacity, supply and last-mile routes, checkpoints, closures, travel times, seasonal constraints, alternatives, optimisation, distance, transport cost, shipment tracking, bottlenecks, pre-positioning, logistics-access maps, field-team routes, warehouse and distribution reports.

## 11. Accessibility and road networks

Road condition, road class and quality, damaged and closed roads, bridges, fords, seasonal roads, checkpoints, access controls, restrictions, distance, travel time, walking and vehicle access, isolated communities, reachable areas, shortest and safest routes, alternatives, bottlenecks, transport availability, market/service access, accessibility maps, road reports, convoy and delivery planning, humanitarian-access constraints.

## 12. Protection and safety

Protection incidents, violence, conflict, mines and explosive remnants, dangerous areas, checkpoints, detention and reception facilities, trafficking routes, GBV providers, child protection, legal assistance, safe spaces, referrals, restricted movement, forced displacement, civilian incidents, aid-worker risks, facility threats, incident patterns, geographic and temporal trends, high-risk populations, confidential maps, safe programming.

## 13. Conflict and security analysis

Conflict events, armed-group activity, attacks, fatalities, front lines, areas of control, territorial change, checkpoints, military positions, explosive incidents, aid-operation security, restrictions, curfews, displacement, intensity, trends, hotspots, proximity analysis, operational risk, satellite monitoring, security briefings, conflict reports, scenarios and enhanced-security priorities.

## 14. Natural hazards and disaster risk

Flood hazard, extent, depth and frequency; drought; wildfire; earthquake; landslide; volcano; cyclone; storm surge; tsunami; heat; cold; avalanche; erosion; coastal hazards; hazard-prone settlements; exposed infrastructure; vulnerable populations; location-based risk; exposure maps; early-warning maps; evacuation and shelter maps; scenarios; regional comparison; disaster-risk assessment; preparedness.

## 15. Climate and environmental mapping

## 16. Remote sensing and satellite imagery

## 17. Drone and aerial mapping

## 19. OpenStreetMap and crowdsourced mapping

## 21. Spatial analysis

## 22. Data analysis and statistics

## 23. Humanitarian needs assessment

Affected populations, severity, priority needs, district comparison, sector needs, underserved communities, household vulnerability, basic-service access, shelter, food, WASH, health, education, protection, livelihoods and early recovery, greatest-risk groups, unmet needs, assistance coverage, resources versus needs, priority rankings, multisector reports and planning inputs.

## 24. Humanitarian programme monitoring

## Typical final outputs

- Printed operational, briefing, hazard, damage, population, service, camp, logistics and accessibility maps
- PDF maps, situation reports, assessment reports, needs-overview reports and donor reports
- Interactive web maps, dashboards, story maps, data portals and geographic profiles
- Vector layers: Shapefile, GeoPackage, GeoJSON and other approved GIS formats
- Raster products, satellite interpretations, orthophotos, DEMs and drone orthomosaics
- Facility inventories, population estimates, route plans and service directories
- Risk, vulnerability, severity and composite indexes
- Priority-area rankings and decision-support analyses
- Statistical tables, charts, infographics, map books and atlases
- Data-quality reports, methodology notes, metadata, data dictionaries and processing lineage
- Training materials, mapping guidelines, SOPs and data-sharing procedures
