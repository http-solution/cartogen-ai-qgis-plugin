# Code of Conduct

Cartogen AI is used by analysts and humanitarian teams. We want contributing to it to be respectful
and useful for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable. Bug reports should say what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
- Never post real personal data, credentials, or sensitive humanitarian data in issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or, for something that should not be
public, open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/http-solution/cartogen-ai-qgis-plugin/security/advisories/new)
and say it is a conduct report. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content, and may lock threads or block
users who do not follow this code.

This code applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where the project is represented.
